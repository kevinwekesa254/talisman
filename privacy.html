Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data The Talisman (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

The Talisman, 320 Ngong Road, Karen, Nairobi, Kenya. For anything about your data, email hello@talisman.co.ke or call +254 705 999 997.

What we collect

When you send us a table request through the form on our home page, we collect:

  • your name and phone number;
  • your preferred date and the number of guests;
  • anything you choose to tell us in the message, such as a celebration, a dietary need or a request for a high chair.

Please don’t share sensitive information, such as health details, beyond what we need to look after you at the table. If you mention an allergy, we use it only to prepare for your visit.

How we use it

  • To call you back, confirm your table and prepare for your visit.
  • To answer questions you send us.

We use your data because you asked us to act on your request. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have separately agreed to hear from us.

Who we share it with

Only with our own team and with the companies that host and run this website for us, under agreements that require them to keep it secure and confidential. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep table requests only as long as we need them to arrange and follow up on your visit, and normally delete them within 12 months.

Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To show our map and typefaces, your browser loads map images and fonts from third-party services, which receive your IP address as part of the normal working of the internet. Links to our Instagram and Facebook pages take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or stop using it;
  • object to how we use it.

Email hello@talisman.co.ke and we will reply within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with our reply, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
