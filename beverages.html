The Bar

Cut from
the garden.

The mint, lemongrass and lime go into the glass within a few minutes of leaving the bed they grew in. The Dawa — vodka, honey, lime and crushed ice, in a gold-rimmed glass — is the house drink and the correct first order.

Beyond that: a short wine list chosen to sit well outdoors, coffee roasted here in Kenya, and a shelf of whisky, gin and rum worth asking to see.
