The Bar

Once the meal
is over.

When the plates have gone, come and wind down at the bar. A glass of something good, the fire going, and no particular reason to leave — it is the best hour of the evening and the one most people forget to plan for.

The mint, lemongrass and lime go into the glass within minutes of leaving the bed they grew in. Beyond that: a short wine list chosen to sit well outdoors, coffee roasted here in Kenya, and a shelf of whisky, gin and rum worth asking to see.
