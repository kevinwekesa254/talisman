Elegance & Ambience

An evening made
of small details.

The Talisman is an old Karen house that never quite stopped being one — stained glass throwing colour across the walls, collected art in every corner, and lanterns coming on as the light goes. The food is cooked the long way round: herbs and greens from our own farm, salmon cured and smoked here over one slow fire, bread baked at dawn. What holds it together is a dedication that rarely announces itself, and a table nobody will ever ask you to give up.
