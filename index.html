About
The Talisman

A talisman is something you keep close, because it holds a quiet kind of magic. For generations of Nairobi, this garden has been that.

Not a restaurant so much as an evening. You come for lunch and stay past sunset. The kids find the garden, the grown-ups find the fireplace, and nobody watches the clock.
