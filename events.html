The Art

New walls,
every month.

The walls here are rehung with a different artist every month. Everything is the work of a living artist, and almost all of it is for sale.

Live Nights

The band starts
when the plates clear.

Jazz and soul by the fireplace, close enough to feel. No stage, no tickets — just musicians in the room and an evening that refuses to end. Once the tables clear, the DJs take it on and the garden keeps going.

Dates on Instagram

Film screenings, book launches and readings run through the year alongside the music. Dates go up on Instagram — they tend to fill quickly.
