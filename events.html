The Art

New walls,
every month.

The walls here are rehung with a different artist every month. One month it is oils and portraiture, the next photography or something a good deal stranger — and the room changes character completely each time.

Everything hanging is the work of a living artist and almost all of it is for sale. Opening nights are open to anyone who happens to be here; no invitation, no guest list. Ask any of the team and they will tell you whose work you are eating under.

Live Nights

The band starts
when the plates clear.

Jazz and soul by the fireplace, close enough to feel. No stage, no tickets — just musicians in the room and an evening that refuses to end. Once the tables clear, the DJs take it on.

Jazz by the fire Friday evenings
Soul & blues sessions Last Saturdays
DJ nights, until late Monthly

Events & Musicfilm nights, launches and late sets

Film screenings, book launches and readings run through the year alongside the music. Dates go up on Instagram — they tend to fill quickly.
