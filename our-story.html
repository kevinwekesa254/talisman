Our Story

An iconic location,
and a light-bulb moment.

Opened at the end of 2003 by two people who had never run a restaurant.

How it began

A talisman, bought
above a shop door.

Sharly and Ian Cameron traded kilims and carved furniture. In a shop in Afghanistan, Sharly spotted a talisman above the door: to ward off evil and bring good luck.

How it grew

Five years, they promised. More than twenty years later, it is still here.

  1. Before 2003

    Traders, not restaurateurs

    Carpets, kilims and carved pillars, and the talisman above a shop door.

  2. June 2003

    The light-bulb moment

    Driving home with an empty fridge, they found the old plot was free.

  3. End of 2003

    Talisman opens

    The pillars came out of storage. Five years, they promised each other.

  4. 2007

    Number one in Nairobi

    Ranked the city’s number one restaurant.

  5. The handover

    Passed into good hands

    Two regulars, Satyan and Stue, bought it and run it still.

  6. Today

    An iconic location

    The original fireplace and painted wall remain, with many of the first staff.

“I still love going there, and many of the original staff are there today to welcome one and all.”

Sharly Cameron · Co-founder

Where we
are now.

An iconic location

Still on Ngong Road, in the same house, with the fireplace and painted wall intact.

Eclectic cuisine

Food from our own farm and from kitchens around the world, with chef’s specials daily.

Art & music

Local artists on the walls and live music by the fire, all through the year.
